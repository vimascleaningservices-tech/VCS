Can every stain be removed?+

Results vary with fabric, stain type, age and previous treatments. Share a clear photo of any marks so VIMAS can advise on likely results. Complete stain removal is not guaranteed.

How long will the fabric take to dry?+

Drying depends on the material, ventilation and weather. Ask for an estimate when booking and follow the team’s advice before using your furniture again.

What areas do you cover?+

VIMAS serves Klang Valley. Send your neighbourhood or postcode via WhatsApp to confirm service coverage and available appointments.

Are the solutions suitable for my home?+

VIMAS uses safe cleaning solutions and professional equipment. Tell the team about delicate fabrics, sensitivities, children or pets so they can advise on suitability and aftercare.
